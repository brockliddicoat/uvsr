# Contributor Agreement Privacy Notice

Version 1.0.0, effective August 9, 2026

## Controller and Contact

Brock Liddicoat administers UVSR's copy of contributor-agreement records and
can be reached at
[brockliddicoat@gmail.com](mailto:brockliddicoat@gmail.com). GitHub and CLA
Assistant separately control data they process for their own services under
their terms.

## Information Collected

The UVSR Contributor License Agreement signing flow records the signer's GitHub
identity, legal name, email address, agreement version,
ownership-and-adulthood confirmation, acceptance response, and signing time.
Pull-request and commit identities are processed to determine which
contributors are covered.

## Purpose

The information is used to verify contributor authority, preserve evidence of
the agreement, administer pull-request checks, contact signers about their
agreement, and protect UVSR and its users from unclear contribution rights. It
is not collected for advertising.

## Processors and Visibility

GitHub authenticates signers and hosts repository activity. The hosted CLA
Assistant service processes signatures and status checks. Its operator controls
the service's infrastructure, security, storage location, and retention under
its own privacy terms. The public agreement and its versions are visible;
signature records are retained as legal evidence and may be exported for
independent preservation.

Providers may process data outside the signer's jurisdiction. Their terms and
privacy notices govern their transfer mechanisms and service-side processing.

Do not put a home address, private employer information, or other unnecessary
personal data in a public pull-request comment.

## Access, Correction, and Deletion

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of UVSR-controlled agreement data,
email [brockliddicoat@gmail.com](mailto:brockliddicoat@gmail.com). Some records
may need to be retained to establish licenses already relied upon, comply with
law, resolve disputes, or protect legal claims. Requests concerning data
controlled by GitHub or CLA Assistant must also be directed to that provider.

## Retention

UVSR-controlled signing evidence is retained while UVSR, a distributed version,
or a licensee may rely on the contribution grant and afterward as reasonably
needed for limitation periods, disputes, audits, and legal compliance. A fixed
deletion date is not promised because the duration of that reliance varies.

Depending on applicable law, a signer may have additional objection,
restriction, portability, complaint, or appeal rights. Use the contact above to
request the applicable process.

## Changes

Material changes receive a new notice version. A changed contributor agreement
requires separate acceptance; changing this privacy notice does not silently
change the signed license grant.

This notice should receive qualified privacy review before collecting
signatures from jurisdictions with additional disclosure requirements.
