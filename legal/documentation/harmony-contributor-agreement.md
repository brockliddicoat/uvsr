## UVSR Relationship

UVSR's contributor agreement states that its structure is informed by Harmony,
uses a copyright license rather than assignment, and follows Harmony's Option
Five outbound-licensing model. The UVSR agreement is project-specific text;
Harmony does not administer it, and this record does not claim that every clause
is copied from or approved by Harmony.
