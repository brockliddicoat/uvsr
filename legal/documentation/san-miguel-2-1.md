## UVSR Relationship

UVSR converts Guillermo M. Leal Llaguno's full-detail scene from OBJ to glTF,
retains source textures, removes invalid geometry the importer cannot preserve,
and applies documented opaque fallbacks to unsupported transparency and
transmission. This is a substantial modified asset derivative.
