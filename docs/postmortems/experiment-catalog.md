# experiment verdict catalog

this catalog separates the mechanisms hidden inside the older UVSR delta postmortems. one historical document often contained several experiments with different outcomes, so the document itself does not receive one blanket verdict.

the old AO ledger contains 414 inventory rows. many were papers, source candidates, forecasts, controls, or unbuilt ideas rather than executed experiments. calling all 414 successful or failed experiments would be false. this catalog groups those rows by the distinct hypothesis they tested. the immutable ledger remains the item-level source.

## verdict language

| verdict | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **flawed as framed** | the stated mechanism cannot satisfy its stated contract. another language, graphics API, prompt, or stronger model does not repair the missing information or contradiction. a materially different contract would be a new experiment. |
| **redesign before retry** | the underlying technique may work, but the experiment bundled too much scope, had no interpretable control, or put ownership in the wrong place. a better prompt alone is insufficient. |
| **retryable with a harness** | the hypothesis remains credible. retry only with a smaller implementation, decisive oracle, fixed conditions, and an owner able to diagnose the relevant layer. |
| **sound or strategically retired** | available evidence does not show a technical failure. the path was corrected, simplified, or removed because its product value, sequence, or lifetime cost did not justify retaining it. |
| **insufficient evidence** | the records do not support an honest success or failure verdict. source, compile, smoke, or static instruction evidence stopped before the decisive test. |

“flawed as framed” applies to the exact contract below, not to every technique sharing its name. “retryable” is not a recommendation to restore old code. it means a clean experiment could answer a useful question.

## quick separation

| verdict | experiments |
| --- | ---: |
| flawed as framed | 24 |
| redesign before retry | 22 |
| retryable with a harness | 17 |
| sound or strategically retired | 24 |
| insufficient evidence | 6 |
| **total** | **93** |

the first group should not be retried under the same contract. the second needs a different scope or ownership model before code. the third can justify a clean prototype once its fixture and oracle exist. the fourth should not be mislabeled as failed, and the fifth needs new evidence before anyone argues for or against it.

## what a stronger model can and cannot change

a stronger model may implement a sound algorithm more faithfully, trace ownership faster, or design a better test. it cannot change an information limit or make contradictory evidence valid. a better prompt is useful only when it narrows the hypothesis, names the owner, supplies the missing fixture and oracle, and gives the agent a stop condition.

the highest-value retries are the ones with a small reference and a text-readable harness: one-signal SVGF, a minimal TAA resolver, HZB traversal including build cost, one ReSTIR strategy, one unlit sky orbit, or one bounded screen-space estimator against a ray-traced control. restoring an old feature family with its controls and compatibility surface would repeat the scope failure before testing the idea.
